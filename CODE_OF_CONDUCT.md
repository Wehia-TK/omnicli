# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Omni CLI to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Show empathy toward other people in the community.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub ([@Wehia-TK](https://github.com/Wehia-TK)). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code.
